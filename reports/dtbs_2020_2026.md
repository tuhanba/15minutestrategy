## Verdict

Break-even at 1.5R needs ≈ 40 % wins before costs; DTBS won 35 % (2020–2023) and 41 % (2024–2026).
No variant reaches the acceptance bar (PF ≥ 1.30, D-034). 2020–2026 total: about −26k on 100k.
DTBS as specified has **no measurable edge**; it should not be traded for the challenge.
